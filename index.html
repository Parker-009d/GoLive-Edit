Designed for a direct local workflow

Your computer runs the mix.

Phone remote on your own network

Connect the phone and host on the same trusted Wi-Fi or hotspot. A private-certificate warning may appear in the phone browser; use only the address shown by GoLive Edit on your own network.

Local Live audio stays local

Local Live sends sound to the output selected on the host. To route that mix into OBS, configure OBS to capture the output or a virtual audio route. No audio travels over the phone connection.

Questions people ask

Quick answers.

Can I use GoLive Edit without OBS?

Yes. Local Live works as a standalone mixer for acoustic sessions, practice, and podcast recording. OBS is optional.

Does the phone remote send my audio over Wi-Fi?

No. Audio stays on the host computer; the phone connection carries controls and meter/analyzer data.

Do I need Rust or Cargo to install the Windows app?

No. Download the standalone Windows EXE. WebView2 is required and is included with most current Windows installations.

Where can I get help or report a problem?

Visit the GitHub Issues page and include your operating system, app version, audio interface, and steps to reproduce.
